My take

Structure Tabs at top let buyers filter by type (articles, notes, tools, frameworks, playbooks) instead of by topic. Featured Substack callout up top for your "3 AI apps I can't live without" piece. Tools section sits visually distinct in ink-navy so it feels like a different category — not just another grid of articles. Quick Notes block (warm cream) bridges the two. Long-form articles at the bottom in their original cream grid.

Tool cards Video placeholder (16:10), tag, name, why-I-use-it, price, "Try X →" CTA, and a small italic "* Affiliate link" disclosure where applicable. F28 Portal example has no affiliate note since it's yours. Click anywhere on the card opens the video; the "Try X →" is the affiliate CTA.

Quick notes vs articles Quick notes are 3-5 minute reads, more personal/observational, cross-posted from Substack. Articles are longer, more structured (frameworks, playbooks, deep dives). The "3 AI apps I can't live without" is the right kind of piece to lead with as featured — short, sharable, links to your tools, perfect Substack subscribe-driver.

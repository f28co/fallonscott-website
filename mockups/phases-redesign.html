Where are you actually?

Three phases. Self-identify into the one that sounds like your week. The work I do follows your readiness, because you can't optimize what isn't documented, and you can't fund what hasn't been optimized.

PHASE 01 — Build

You're building v1.

Are you Hire-Ready… or hiring on vibes because your onboarding is broken?

You're here if

  • The business lives in your head, or in 2–3 key people's heads.
  • No SOPs. No org chart. No documented customer journey or process maps.
  • Hiring is reactive. Onboarding is whatever you remember to say that week.
  • Turnover is high. You keep blaming "bad hires" or saying no one can do it like you, when the real problem is upstream.
  • Everything routes through you. The day you stop, the business stops.
What we build together

The v1 written down. Process maps, role clarity, and the documents that make you replaceable.

→ Start with The SOP Suite (DIY) or Foundations (with me)
PHASE 02 — Optimize

You're refining the version that grows.

Are you Growth-Ready… or about to scale a broken system?

You're here if

  • You have processes, but they're slow, leaky, or shaped around the wrong people.
  • Tools and platforms don't talk to each other. Tech stacked from a dozen gurus.
  • You're refining, but not sure if it aligns with where the business should actually go.
  • You need a strategic operator who starts with the end in mind, not just a workflow tweaker.
What we build together

Right people, right tools, right sequence, all architected against the destination you're driving toward.

→ Start with the Optimization Engagement
PHASE 03 — Scale or Sale

You're ready for capital.

Are you Investor-Ready and Exit-Ready… or just running on instinct?

You're here if

  • Foundation is solid, operations are tight, but you don't have full visibility.
  • No KPI dashboards. No monthly rhythm. No clean numbers to show a buyer or investor.
  • You're ready to deploy capital, pitch investors, or prepare for a clean exit.
  • You need diligence-ready ops. Defensible margins. A business that doesn't depend on you.
What we build together

Fundable, scalable, sellable operations. The version a buyer or investor can actually read under the hood.

→ Start with the Fractional COO Engagement
The loop You can't scale chaos. You can't sell a business that depends on you. Capital won't deploy into either. So we go in order: build, optimize, fund, free.

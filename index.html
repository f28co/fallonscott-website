07 / Real questions

Quick answers, real conversations.

A few of the questions I get most often before a discovery call.

Who is this actually for?

Founder-led businesses where too much still runs through the founder or one or two key people. Most of our clients have 10 to 100 people, and where you start depends on your stage. Earlier-stage companies, often under $1M in revenue, usually start with an Operational Assessment or a targeted Acceleration: one area our team comes in and fixes. Companies that are scaling, preparing to raise capital or planning an exit tend to need a full Operational Transformation. Fractional COO leadership, where I step into the operating seat myself, is for businesses that need a senior operator.

How long do engagements take, and what do they cost?

Every engagement is scoped after a discovery call, because the price depends on what your business needs, not a package tier. As a rough guide, a targeted Acceleration runs 4 to 12 weeks. A full Transformation runs 6 to 18 months, depending on company size and scope. Fractional COO leadership is an ongoing retainer. You won't get a number on the first call. You'll get clarity on what the work involves, then a scoped proposal.

How fast can you start?

It depends on what you need. The F28 & Co. team runs multiple engagements at once and can usually kick off within 1 to 2 weeks of an accepted proposal. Fractional COO leadership works differently. I hold two of those seats at a time so each client gets real depth, so start dates depend on when a seat opens.

09 / What's actually at stake

Build the system, or build a job you own.

No middle option. Either the business runs without you, or you run it until you can't anymore.

WITHOUT THE SYSTEM

Here's what it's costing you.

  • Capital won't deploy. Investors and lenders pass on businesses that depend on the founder.
  • You can't scale past yourself. Every new client adds load to the same one person.
  • Hiring is a gamble. Wrong people, wrong seats, no rubric, no onboarding.
  • Turnover is a tax. Every loss costs three months of someone else's productivity.
  • Marketing and sales live in your head. The day you stop showing up, revenue stops.
  • There's no exit. Just a job you happen to own, and one you can't sell.
WITH THE SYSTEM

Here's what becomes possible.

  • Capital deploys. Lenders, investors, and acquirers see a real business, not a personality.
  • You scale on margin, not on hours. Headcount adds output, not chaos.
  • Hiring becomes repeatable. Right person, right seat, every time.
  • Retention follows. People know what they're walking into and what success looks like.
  • Marketing and sales run with or without you. Revenue stops being a personality test.
  • You get a graceful exit, on your terms. Stay, step back, or walk away cleanly.

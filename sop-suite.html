03 / The 2026 question every founder is asking

Before you hire anyone, human or AI, you need to know what the job actually is.

The SOP Suite answers that question first. Once you know what the work is, what good looks like, and how it gets done, then you decide who (or what) does it.

Decision 01 AI employee

Repetitive. Rule-based. High-volume.

Data entry, ticket triage, inbox sorting, invoice coding, calendar logistics, content reformatting, lead qualification on stated criteria. If a clear rulebook can do the job, an AI agent should.

Decision 02 Human

Judgment. Relationship. Nuance.

Difficult client conversations, hiring decisions, creative direction, exceptions to the rules, negotiation, escalations. Anything that fails when the answer is "it depends." Humans only.

Decision 03 Human, managing AI

Both. The hybrid seat.

Most modern operations roles. A human reviews, approves, exception-handles, and trains; the AI executes the volume work underneath. The leverage seat, and the one most teams should be staffing for now.

05 / The three steps to delegation

Three steps to stress-free delegation.
Most founders skip the first two.

Delegation is a three-step system, in order: Dump, Document, Delegate. The reason most teams fail isn't laziness or bad hires. It's that they jump straight to step three, or they half-finish step one and call it done. The order matters. So does the depth.

01

Dump

Get the whole business out of your head, the founder's head, the operator's head, every key stakeholder. Picture a 1,000-piece puzzle still in the box. You can't sort it until you tip it onto the floor. This step has three movements, in order.

The Dump phase, in three moves

1A · OFFLOAD
Offload

Every task, decision, and dependency, written down. The masterlist. See the whole business on one page.

1B · ORGANIZE
Organize

Map every step end to end, in order, across the 7 C's of the client journey, plus HR, finance and admin. See who does what, on which platform, and where the gaps are.

1C · OPTIMIZE
Optimize

Before you document, find where your map needs a second look. Optimizing is a separate, paid review our team does with clients: what to eliminate, consolidate, delegate, and where automation and AI earn their place.

02

Document

Capture the optimized process in a real SOP, not a Loom buried in Slack. Tools, time, owner, inputs, outputs, edge cases. The format matters as much as the content. If a brand-new hire (or AI agent) can't follow it without you in the room, it isn't documented yet.

03

Delegate

Slot the right person, human or AI, into the seat the SOP defines. Onboard them through the documentation. Step out without it falling apart. This is the only step most founders attempt, which is why most delegation fails.

Where most founders break They dump partially, skip the organize and optimize moves, document at 30,000 feet (no specifics, no screenshots, no videos), then race to hire because they're drowning. The new person can't follow what was never properly written, the founder takes the work back, and everyone calls it a "bad hire."

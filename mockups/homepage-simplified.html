Where are you actually?

You likely fall in one of three buckets. Pick the question that lands hardest and start there.

PHASE 01 — Build

Are you Hire-Ready?

Or hiring on vibes because your onboarding is broken?

  • Business lives in your head, or 2–3 key people's heads
  • No SOPs, no org chart, no documented customer journey
  • Turnover is high. You keep blaming "bad hires"
  • Everything routes through you
→ Start with The SOP Suite or Foundations
PHASE 02 — Optimize

Are you Growth-Ready?

Or about to scale a broken system?

  • Processes are slow, leaky, or shaped around the wrong people
  • Tools don't talk. Tech stacked from a dozen gurus
  • You're refining, but not against a destination
  • You need an architect, not a workflow tweaker
→ Start with the Optimization Engagement
PHASE 03 — Scale or Sale

Are you Investor-Ready?

Or running your business on heroics?

  • Foundation solid, ops tight, but no full visibility
  • No KPI dashboards or data-driven decision layers
  • Ready to deploy capital, pitch investors, or prep for exit
  • You need diligence-ready ops
→ Start with the Fractional COO Engagement

Audit. Build. Optimize.

Three steps. The order matters. Most founders and most freelance operators skip the first one.

01

Audit the gaps.

An auditor's eye finds the root cause. Where time, cash, and attention are actually leaking.

02

Build the systems.

Process maps. SOPs. Automations. AI agents. The right tool for the right gap.

03

Optimize for both.

Refine for productivity and profitability. Systems that get cheaper and faster the longer they run.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Fallon Scott ("we," "I," "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit fallonscott.com or engage with our services.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, company, message content when you fill out a contact form, book a discovery call, subscribe to a lead magnet, or purchase a digital product.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring URL, and timestamps via standard analytics.
  • Cookies: we use minimal cookies for site functionality and aggregated analytics. We do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver requested services or products.
  • To send marketing emails you've opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve the website and understand visitor behavior in aggregate.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (email, payment processing, hosting, analytics) — bound by contract to protect your data.
  • Authorities when legally required.

4. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing hello@fallonscott.com. EU/UK/California residents have additional rights under GDPR/CCPA — contact us to exercise them.

5. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions? Email hello@fallonscott.com.
